Your TikTok videos and stats in one place

Natt TikTok App is a personal web tool. You sign in with your TikTok account, see your profile numbers and your public videos, and send new videos to TikTok as drafts.

  • Sign in with TikTokUses TikTok Login Kit. We never see your TikTok password.
    user.info.basic
  • Profile and statsBio, profile link, followers, following, likes and video count.
    user.info.profile · user.info.stats
  • Your videosA list of your public videos with views and likes.
    video.list
  • Upload draftsPick a video file and send it to your TikTok inbox to finish and post in the TikTok app.
    video.upload

How it works

  1. Click Continue with TikTok and approve the permissions TikTok shows you.
  2. Your dashboard loads your profile, stats and recent videos.
  3. To upload, choose a video file. It arrives in TikTok as a draft; you edit and post it yourself.
  4. You can disconnect at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Natt TikTok App (“the App”) collects when you sign in with TikTok, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information we receive from TikTok

Only with your permission, through TikTok’s official API:

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar (user.info.basic).
  • Profile details: bio, profile link, verified status (user.info.profile).
  • Statistics: follower, following, likes and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • Your public videos and their view and like counts (video.list).

We do not receive your TikTok password.

2. Videos you upload

When you choose a video to upload, the file is sent to TikTok as a draft (video.upload). We do not keep a copy after the transfer finishes.

3. How we use information

Only to show you your own dashboard and to perform uploads you request. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except TikTok itself as needed to provide the features.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to call TikTok’s API for you. Profile and video data is fetched when you open the dashboard and is not kept longer than needed to display it.

5. Your choices

You can revoke the App’s access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions. You can also email us to ask that any data related to you be deleted; we will do so within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.
